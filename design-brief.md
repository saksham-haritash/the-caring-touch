# Design Brief: The Caring Touch (Dental & Eye Care)

## 1. Business Identity
- **Business:** The Caring Touch
- **Category:** Multi-specialty Dental & Eye Clinic
- **Location:** New Rajinder Nagar, New Delhi
- **Target Customer:** Local residents, premium patients seeking specialized care in a single location.
- **Conversion Goal:** Appointment bookings and inquiry calls.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book Appointment" (Sticky/Floating)
- **Secondary CTA:** "Call Now" / "WhatsApp Us"

## 2. Visual Style & Branding
- **Style:** Ultra-premium, Medical-Modern, Trustworthy, Airy.
- **Colors:** 
  - Primary: Deep Medical Blue (`#0F172A`) and Clean Teal/Cyan (`#06B6D4`)
  - Accent: Soft White and Light Grey for clean backgrounds.
- **Typography:** 
  - Headers: Inter or Montserrat (Bold, clean, professional).
  - Body: Open Sans or Inter (Highly readable).

## 3. Structure & Content
- **Hero Section:** High-impact headline "Complete Care for Your Eyes & Smile," subtitled with trust indicators (since 2000). Strong dual-CTA.
- **Services Grid:** 
  - Family Dentistry
  - Cosmetic Dentistry
  - Comprehensive Eye Care
  - Cardiac Care / Aviation Medicine (Secondary focus)
- **Why Choose Us:** Highlighting expert doctors, 20+ years of experience, and multi-specialty convenience.
- **The Process:** Consult -> Diagnose -> Treatment -> Follow-up.
- **Testimonial Section:** Premium slider with star ratings.
- **FAQ:** Common dental and eye care questions.
- **Contact/Footer:** Map integration, direct call/whatsapp buttons, address.

## 4. Animation Plan (Motion)
- **Hero:** Text fade-in and slide-up; subtle floating effect for medical assets.
- **Services:** Staggered reveal as user scrolls into view.
- **Cards:** Soft lift-on-hover effect.
- **CTAs:** Pulse animation for the "Book Now" sticky button on mobile.
- **Page Transitions:** Smooth section-by-section entry.

## 5. Asset Plan
- **Images:** Use Gemini to generate premium, photorealistic interiors of a modern dental/eye clinic and professional-looking medical staff (generic but high-quality).
- **Icons:** Lucide-React (Dental, Eye, Heart, Clock, Map-Pin).
